# Final Polish Checklist

This checklist is for the last refinement pass before treating the homepage as a production-ready academic site.

## Must Fix

- Recheck the initial `Home` anchor state in Chrome, Edge, and direct `#home` loads to ensure the first screen always lands in the intended composition.
- Re-verify sticky navigation behavior across long scrolling and section jumps so the active label never lags or desynchronizes.
- Do one final pass on `News` spacing, divider weight, and page controls so the right column feels clean instead of slightly crowded.
- Normalize member portrait crops once the final assets arrive; keep the current portrait system, but tune framing per person instead of forcing one crop rule.
- Run one more browser-performance pass in Edge with the final content set, focusing on the footer field, backdrop-filter areas, and initial page load.

## Should Improve

- Tighten section summaries so they read faster; the site works best when each section opens with one hard, short statement.
- Reduce small inconsistencies in card density between `Publications`, `Projects`, `Systems`, and `Awards`, so the homepage feels more uniformly authored.
- Confirm the visual weight of the `News` lead item after final content is inserted; the first item should guide attention without overpowering the center monument.
- Recheck footer balance after final institutional text and ICP information are inserted; the current footer works, but real text may shift its rhythm.
- Perform a final typography audit for line breaks in the hero title and long publication/project titles at common laptop widths.

## Optional Enhancements

- Add one stronger but disciplined "signature moment" on first arrival, such as a very subtle settling of the monogram and monument layers during page load.
- Give section changes slightly more environmental distinction through tiny shifts in peripheral atmosphere, not through scaling or obvious animation.
- Treat the footer coordination band as a secondary identity system and refine it until it feels unique to the site rather than simply decorative.
- Consider one more pass on tonal contrast so the monument remains the clear visual ruler even when surrounding content grows denser.

## Final Standard

The site is ready when:

- the monument stays dominant,
- the navigation always feels precise,
- the right-column signal area feels deliberate,
- the lower sections no longer feel weaker than the first screen,
- and the whole page reads as one authored world rather than a strong hero followed by normal sections.
